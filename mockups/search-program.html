LEAP TO LEGACY

What carries over, what changes

Same as Leap

  • Ten-dimension audit with score, confidence, evidence, and business impact
  • OBSERVED, INFERRED, UNVERIFIED on every finding
  • 30/60/90 roadmap and a printable checklist
  • KPI dashboard, reported monthly
  • Monthly AI prompt panel for GEO

Different for Legacy

  • Local matters. Elk Grove LocalBusiness schema and the Google Business Profile (ownership unconfirmed)
  • Peter signs off on every technical line before it's indexed
  • The tradecraft wall. Pages say what the Legacy Standard checks, never how
  • Authority comes from the paddock: race results, NASA and BMW CCA coverage, community answers
  • No competitor names, in copy or in the public audit
OPEN CALLS

Four decisions for LJ

Weekend cost: NASA's $1,500 to $2,000 or Peter's $3,000 to $4,000 all in?The hub can carry one figure cleanly. Two figures need two labels.

RUSH SR and SPEC Boxster: hub pages later, or drop them from the homepage tagline?Claiming three classes with one deep page dilutes the Spec E46 signal.

Who owns the Google Business Profile for Elk Grove?Local search and AI Overviews both read it. Needs a login check.

The "West Coast's most documented Spec E46 builder" line: approve it or not?AI engines repeat a clear one-line claim. Until it's approved, the hub runs on "Built to the Legacy Standard" alone.
