What it costs to race Spec E46

By Peter Jones, Legacy Motorworks · PUBLISH_DATE
Short answer

A used Spec E46 runs $35K to $65K. A new car built ready to race runs $65K to $90K. Then there's the race weekend, every weekend. If you're building in phases, a used car is the right way in.

Most guys price the car and forget the weekend. The car is the number everybody asks about. The weekend is the number that decides whether you're still racing next season.

So here's both.

That spread is real and it comes down to what's fresh and what isn't. Two cars with the same paint and the same number on the door can be very different cars underneath (the cage, the suspension, how many race weekends the engine has on it, whether the old brackets got cut out or painted over).

The price tells you less than the paperwork does. Ask what's fresh. Then ask what isn't. A seller who answers the second question as plainly as the first is the one to keep talking to.

The weekend

A race weekend all in runs $3,000 to $4,000 [PJ: confirm weekend figure, open call 01]. That's entry fees, fuel, tires, the tow, and the hotel. Race a full season and it adds up faster than the car does.

There are only a handful of racing weekends every year. The hall pass to go racing, the cost to tow to the track, stay in a hotel, entry fees etc. are all wasted when a small part fails. It is very much a pay now or pay later.

That's why a cheap car that needs work isn't cheap. You pay for the part either way. The only question is whether you also pay for the weekend it ruined.

The cheaper ways in

Now I have to say, not everyone should start with a race-ready car. If you're doing it in phases and can't do it all at once, a budget build or a used car is the right call. We're not the only way into this sport and I'd never tell you we were.

And if you just want to find out whether you like it, there's 24 Hours of Lemons, a series built around cars worth next to nothing. I haven't raced it, I've been involved. It's mostly friends bonding over a project and spending the weekend keeping the car running. Not a bad way to learn.

If you've never done it

Road racing is hilly backroads in a loop. With 40 other cars. Spec E46 means everybody runs the same BMW E46 built to the same class rules (those rules come from NASA, not from us), so it comes down to prep and the driver.

At the end of the day

The car is a one time number. The weekend is every time. Budget for the weekend FIRST, then buy the car that lets you keep showing up.
