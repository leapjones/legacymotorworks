The part that improves steering feel, and can take your steering away

By Peter Jones, Legacy Motorworks · PUBLISH_DATE
Short answer

Solid control arm bushings sharpen steering feel. They also send all the load and shock into the inner ball joint of the lower control arm. When that joint breaks, you lose all steering input. Better feel isn't always worth the trade.

Over 100 mph, I turned the wheel and nothing happened.

The car sailed off into the desert until it came to rest. As soon as you realize you are no longer in control you brace and hope for the best. That's all there is to do.

Here's what broke, and why I'm careful about any part sold on feel.

What the part does

Solid control arm bushings replace the stock rubber ones. They do improve steering feel. You notice it right away, and plenty of guys love it.

Where the load goes

With the rubber gone, all the load and shock goes into the inner ball joint of the lower control arm. When that breaks you lose all steering input and become a passenger.

It wasn't only my car. I watched the same failure happen at Sonoma, turn 1, uphill, over 100 mph. That's a track with high consequences when you get it wrong.

When the rule got made

Not in the desert. You're busy then. It got made back at the shop, once the car was towed in and I was assessing the damage. That's where a never again comes from. The crash is the event. The rule is what you do with it afterward.
