The Spec E46 Inspection Checklist

What to check on a used Spec E46 race car before money changes hands. Six sections, in the order Peter Jones looks at a car.

Send me the checklist

Why it exists

A used Spec E46 runs $35K to $65K. The listing tells you what's new. It rarely tells you what isn't, and some damage doesn't show until the car's on an alignment rack.

Build guides for Spec E46 exist. A checklist for inspecting a car that's already built didn't, so Legacy made one.

What's in it

  1. Chassis and subframe
  2. Trailing-arm pockets
  3. Control arms
  4. Cooling
  5. Safety and cage
  6. Paperwork and logbook

One page per section, printable, built to take to the car.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't replace hands on the car. It tells you where to look.
  • It won't tell you what a car is worth.
  • It covers public failure points only: what any careful buyer can check.

Building from a street car instead? Start with the class Build Worksheet from the series at spece46.com/resources.

Who it's from

Legacy Motorworks builds Spec E46 race cars in Elk Grove, California. Legacy cars won the 2025 NASA NorCal championship. Before that, Legacy customers won the 2022 NorCal and SoCal championships, and Legacy cars took two of the top three spots at the 2022 Nationals (Performance Racing Industry, 2023).
